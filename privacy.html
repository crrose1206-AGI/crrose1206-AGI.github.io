Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

cIlpz is software that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your information, and what it does not do.

Where your data lives

cIlpz is not a hosted service. It runs on a machine you control, and the videos you give it, the transcripts it makes, and the clips it produces are stored on that machine. We do not operate a server that receives your videos, and we cannot see your files, your clips or your TikTok account.

What cIlpz stores on your computer

  • Videos and clips you send it, and the finished clips it makes.
  • Transcripts of the speech in those videos, used to place subtitles and pick moments. Deleted along with the clip.
  • A job record for each clip (when it ran, whether you approved it), so the app can show its history and statistics.
  • TikTok access tokens, if you connect an account. These are stored in a local file so you do not have to sign in again. Removing the account deletes the file.

You can delete all of it at any time from inside the app.

What cIlpz receives from TikTok

If you connect a TikTok account through TikTok's own login page, cIlpz requests only:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your display name and an account identifier, so the app can show which account a clip will be posted to.
  • Permission to upload a video as a draft (video.upload).
  • Permission to publish a video you have approved (video.publish).

cIlpz does not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages, likes or analytics, and does not change your profile or post anything you have not approved. Before a posting screen is shown, it asks TikTok for your current posting settings (which audiences you may choose, whether comments, Duet or Stitch are switched off, and your maximum video length) so that the choices offered match your account. That information is used only to draw that screen and is not kept.

What is sent to other services

  • TikTok receives a clip and the settings you chose, only when you tap to send it.
  • Telegram carries the messages between you and your own bot, including the clips it sends you for review, under Telegram's privacy policy.
  • An AI provider (Anthropic, using your own API key) receives the transcript text of a clip, and a few still frames from it, to suggest a caption. It does not receive your TikTok data, your account details or the video file.
  • Video sources you give it, such as Twitch or YouTube, are contacted to download the video you asked for.

Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used to train anything.

Keeping and deleting data

Clips awaiting your review, and the source video kept so a cut can be changed, are deleted automatically after a short window you configure. Approved clips can be set to delete themselves after a number of days. Disconnecting a TikTok account deletes its stored token immediately. Uninstalling cIlpz and deleting its folder removes everything.

Children

cIlpz is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by TikTok in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
