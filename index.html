Turn long streams into short vertical clips

cIlpz takes a link or a video file, finds the moment worth keeping, and returns a captioned 9:16 clip. Nothing is published until you look at it and approve it.

How it works

  1. Send a video. A link or an uploaded file, with a note like 12:30–13:10, 3 clips, or the part where they start rapping.
  2. cIlpz cuts it. It transcribes the audio, picks the moment, crops to vertical, writes a headline, and burns in word-by-word subtitles.
  3. You review it. The finished clip comes back with buttons: approve, reject, change the caption, nudge the start or end, try the next best moment, or re-cut by time.
  4. You choose where it goes. Approved clips are saved. If you connect a TikTok account, you can send a clip there as a draft or post it directly.

Accurate subtitles

Speech is transcribed locally with word-level timing, so captions land on the word.

You approve everything

Every clip is reviewed by a person before it can be posted anywhere. There is no automatic publishing you did not switch on yourself.

Editable cuts

Move the start or end a few seconds, cut a dead patch out of the middle, stitch two moments together, or change the speed.

Trending moments

Optionally watch Twitch channels for clips gaining views unusually fast, and turn one into a finished clip with a tap.

How cIlpz uses TikTok

Connecting a TikTok account is optional; cIlpz works fully without it. When you do connect one, through TikTok's own login page:

  • Your account name is read so cIlpz can show which account a clip would be posted to, and so you can connect more than one and choose between them.
  • Send as a draft puts the clip in your TikTok inbox. You finish and publish it yourself in the TikTok app.
  • Post directly shows a screen where you pick who can see the post, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether the post promotes a brand. Nothing is preselected, and nothing is sent until you tap post.

cIlpz never posts on its own. It never changes your TikTok profile, follows anyone, or reads your videos, messages or followers. You can disconnect an account at any time, which deletes the stored access token.

Where it runs

cIlpz runs on your own computer and is operated through a private chat with your own bot. Your videos are processed on that machine: transcription and video editing happen locally, not on someone else's server.
